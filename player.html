    Ad-free audio
    Streams use yt-dlp bestaudio — YouTube player ads are never in the pipe. If a song fails, that is usually an extractor break (auto-updated on start / heal), not an adblocker miss. SponsorBlock (SB) only skips in-video sponsors/intros you choose.
    Import playlist

    YouTube / YouTube Music playlist URL, Spotify playlist/album link, or one song per line ("Artist - Title" or YouTube URLs). Amazon/Apple Music: paste track names. Or load .m3u / .csv / .txt.
